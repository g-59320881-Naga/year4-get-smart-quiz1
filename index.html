Answer Key

  1. A – She broke her leg.
  2. C – In hospital.
  3. B – Stay longer.
  4. A – A cough and a fever.
  5. C – To the hospital.
  6. B – She hurt her leg and it is bleeding.
  7. A – Take some medicine and sleep.
  8. C – Clean her cut and change the plaster.
  9. B – Should.
  10. B – They want to help Anna and spend time with her.
